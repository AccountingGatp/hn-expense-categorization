Bank Expense Categorization → QBO Import

Select a bank/CC account, upload its register + bank transactions → get a categorized QuickBooks import workbook.

🔒 Files are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
Bank Expense Categorization Expense Dashboard ↗

1 Select the Bank / Credit Card account

One bank account per run — QBO imports per bank account. Pick the account this month's register and bank export are both for, or add a new one from your Chart of Accounts.

2 Upload this account's files

Bank export needs at minimum Date, Description, Amount. MSO register export needs Date, Payee, Memo, Class, Payment/Deposit, Account.

Optional — override the standing master lists / QBO template for this run
Select an account and upload both files to enable.

3 Review & confirm (checkpoint)

Every bank transaction lands in exactly one of the two tabs below — nothing is force-mapped or dropped. Fix anything under Needs Review, then confirm before downloading.

🧮
Checkpoint 1 — Reconciliation

QBO Import — rows (Payee / Account / Class are editable dropdowns)

Needs Review — rows the app couldn't confidently categorize

Assign Payee / Account / Class where you can, then click Include to move a row into the QBO Import tab. Rows you leave as-is export to the Excluded - Needs Review tab of the workbook (e.g. credit card payments/transfers are expected here — that's not an error).

✅
Checkpoint 2 — Confirm before download Reviewed the Needs Review tab and happy with the categorization above.
